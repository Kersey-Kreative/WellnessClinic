Our Services

Supporting your path to balance and wellness, every step of the way. From personalized sessions to mindful group experiences, our services are designed to meet you where you are—and help you move forward with clarity and confidence.

We offer one-on-one support for individuals seeking a safe space to process life’s challenges, build resilience, and gain deeper self-awareness. For couples, we provide a collaborative environment to strengthen communication, rebuild trust, and navigate relational stress with compassion and clarity. Group counseling creates space for shared healing—especially for those working through addiction or trauma—by fostering connection, understanding, and growth in community.

Our meditation classes offer a grounding practice for anyone looking to reduce stress, improve emotional regulation, and reconnect with the present moment. Whether you're beginning your journey or continuing your growth, VitalEdge is here to walk with you—offering care that is thoughtful, personalized, and deeply human.

One-On-One Therapy

A space just for you. Personalized support to help you navigate challenges, build resilience, and grow with clarity and confidence.

Couples Counseling

Grow together, not apart. Strengthen communication, rebuild trust, and navigate challenges as a team in a supportive, judgment-free space.

Group Counseling

Healing in community. Supportive group sessions for those navigating addiction recovery or healing from trauma—guided, safe, and connection-driven.

Meditation Classes

Stillness for the soul. Learn practical techniques to reduce stress, increase self-awareness, and cultivate inner peace through guided meditation.
