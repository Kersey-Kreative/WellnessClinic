Our Team

At VitalEdge Wellness Clinic, our team is the heart of everything we do. Each member brings a unique blend of experience, compassion, and expertise—united by a shared commitment to whole-person care. We’re more than clinicians; we’re listeners, guides, and partners in your journey toward balance and well-being.

Whether you're meeting with a licensed counselor, attending a group session, or exploring mindfulness through meditation, you'll find a team that leads with empathy and integrity. We believe healing happens in relationship—and our team is here to create a space where you feel supported, understood, and empowered every step of the way.
